Overview

Amoha Leaf is Amoha Group’s second and most ambitious project — a 115-acre integrated development on Ring Road, Bathinda, with 10 acres dedicated to commercial space and the rest residential. It stands among the most talked-about addresses in the city, planned end-to-end by Studio Symbiosis, Germany, with every element — from the entry gate to the street light poles and park walkways — inspired by the form of a leaf.
