Overview

Amoha Leaf is the group’s second and most ambitious project: a 115-acre integrated development with ten acres dedicated to commercial space and the rest residential. It stands among the most talked-about addresses in the city, planned end to end by Studio Symbiosis, Germany, with every element — from the entry gate to the street-light poles and park walkways — inspired by the form of a leaf.

Key highlights

Design and planning

Master-planned by Studio Symbiosis, Germany, around the motif of a leaf — gates, street lights, walkways and more. The first project in the region with full CCTV coverage.

Roads and connectivity

A 100 ft commercial main entry, an 80 ft residential main road, internal roads of 50 and 60 ft, 20 ft park-to-park walkways and a 35 ft periphery road, running parallel to Canal Road.

Green spaces

Among the largest parks in any project in the region — two acres, one and a half acres and one acre — landscaped for gathering, recreation and daily wellness.

Lifestyle and recreation

One of the biggest clubhouses in the region: badminton, table tennis and snooker indoors; cricket and football outdoors; two pickleball courts, a first for the region; kids’ play area, banquet hall, spa, gym, restaurant, guest rooms and an infinity pool.

Commercial

Ultra-modern architectural elevations behind a 100 ft grand commercial entrance.
