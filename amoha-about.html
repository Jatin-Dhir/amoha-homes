About Amoha Group

Building Bathinda’s future, one landmark at a time.

Premium townships and luxury plots along Bathinda’s Ring Road, from the developers behind ABP Group.

Five years, five landmark projects

Amoha Group was established five years ago, backed by over 30 years of real estate experience and expertise brought by its founders. In a short journey, we have developed 5 landmark projects spanning 170+ acres, with a growing portfolio of thoughtfully planned residential and commercial developments — and served 1000+ satisfied families.

Why the name Amoha

Amoha is derived from Sanskrit, representing purity, clarity, and freedom from illusion. We chose the name because it reflects what we stand for: purity in vision, clarity in approach, and integrity in everything we build.
