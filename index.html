Why the name Amoha

Purity in vision, clarity in approach

Sanskrit

Amoha is derived from Sanskrit, representing purity, clarity, and freedom from illusion. We chose the name because it reflects what we stand for: purity in vision, clarity in approach, and integrity in everything we build.

Master planner

Studio Symbiosis, Germany

Amoha Leaf is master-planned by Studio Symbiosis, Germany, with the entire project designed around the motif of a leaf — gates, street lights, walkways and more — and it is the first project in the region with 100% CCTV coverage.

Amoha Group’s next development is a 55-acre residential-cum-commercial project on Ring Road, Bathinda, located directly opposite Bathinda Textile Market. Full project details to be announced soon.
