Overview

Amoha Group’s first project, Amoha Gardens is a 36-acre township offering primarily residential plots, along with 15 SCOs and 4 booths for commercial use. Plot sizes range from 166 sq yds to 320 sq yds, giving families the flexibility to build a home that fits their needs.
