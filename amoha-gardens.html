Overview

Amoha Gardens offers primarily residential plots, along with fifteen SCOs and four booths for commercial use. Plot sizes range from 166 to 320 square yards, giving families the flexibility to build a home that fits their needs.

Key highlights

On the highway

Situated on National Highway NH-7, near Thermal, with direct access from the highway itself.

Wide roads

A grand 70 ft main entry road, one 60 ft road, and every remaining internal road 40 ft wide.

Six parks

Six parks and green pockets, one near every residential block.

A track for every morning

A 500 m jogging track with both EPDM (soft) and hard flooring — a rare feature in the region.
